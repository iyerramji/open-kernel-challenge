# Publication scope

All candidate source revisions and our runner scripts are included. Exact archived code snapshots remain unchanged. Active runners use a portable project root. Official and Modal results are separate evidence classes.

Downloaded challenge interface/source, website/terms copies, third-party checkouts/wheels, credentials, billing/container snapshots and private handoff notes remain local. Obtain official reference/checks with `okc init muse_bf16_proj problems/muse_bf16_proj` after your own login/terms acceptance. `official/problem.json` here contains benchmark metadata only.

Historical local manifests may name omitted local-only artifacts. They are preserved as provenance, not as a claim that omitted files are published. The export manifest records exact included source/artifact hashes. Candidate comments are historical; the README/results tables provide current validation status.
